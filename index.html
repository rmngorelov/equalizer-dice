Rules

The dice
Die 1
Die 2
Die 1 has every piece once. Die 2 has no king and two pawns.
Your turn
Roll both dice and move a piece of either type shown. If neither type can make a legal move, roll again.
Doubles
Both dice show the same piece. Play any legal move.
Castling
Allowed when it is legal and you rolled a king, a rook, or doubles.
In check
Only a legal answer to the check is allowed. Capture the checking piece, move the king, or block with another piece.
Winning
Checkmate ends the game.
Agree first
The rules leave two things open. Do the dice still limit you while you are in check? And does everyone roll, or only the stronger player? Settle both before the first move.

Odds on any roll

Chance a single roll lets you move each piece. Doubles count, because they allow any legal move.
